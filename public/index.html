How it works

Three steps. Ten seconds.

The original presentation is back. The product underneath is intentionally simpler: paste notes, run extraction, export the output.

STEP 01
Paste your notes
Copy raw meeting notes from anywhere — Docs, Notion, WhatsApp, or a scratchpad. Messy is fine.
STEP 02
Click Extract
ActionPulse reads your notes, pulls tasks, assigns owners when possible, surfaces deadlines, and scores priority.
STEP 03
Copy & share
Export the current result as Markdown or JSON and drop it into the tools your team already uses.
